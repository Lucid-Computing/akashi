# Security reporting

Send vulnerability reports privately to the maintainer at [abdel@lucidcomputing.ai](mailto:abdel@lucidcomputing.ai).

Include the affected component, commit or release, what you observed, the expected behavior and the security property affected. Use synthetic or redacted evidence. Keep credentials, signing keys, model inputs, private capabilities and proof material out of the report.

The maintainer coordinates investigation, remediation and disclosure through the private conversation. The [security model](docs/spec/06-security.md#trust-and-evidence) describes the gateway, log, auditor and holder trust boundaries.
